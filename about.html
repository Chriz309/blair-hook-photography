About

Hi, I’m Chris.

ChrisPhotographer · Potchefstroom

I’m the guy behind the camera at Blair Hook Photography — and usually the one making you laugh so the photos don’t look posed.

My path here wasn’t a straight line. Before photography, I worked as a commercial diver and later as a commercial dive supervisor: long days, tough conditions and absolutely no room for mistakes. I saved up from that work so I could come back and study electromechanical engineering here in Potchefstroom.

Photography is how I get by while I study, and I put everything into it. That diving background taught me to stay calm, plan properly and look after the people I’m working with. Engineering made me obsessive about detail. Put together, it means you get a shoot that’s relaxed on the outside and carefully thought through underneath.

I don’t have a studio, and I don’t want one. I’d rather meet you on campus, in a field at golden hour or wherever your story actually happens. I don’t watch the clock, I give away far more photos than most, and if it’s your graduation or a couple’s shoot, there’s champagne.
