Good to know

Grad questions

How much is a graduation shoot?

Graduation sessions start at R1 500 and include two glasses of champagne. If you’re bringing a bigger group, the group rate of R200 per person applies (minimum R1 500). See sessions & pricing.

Can we shoot on a different day from the ceremony?

Absolutely — many graduates prefer a quieter day before or after, when campus is calmer and nobody is rushing. Just bring the gown.

Can my family and friends be in the photos?

Yes please! Parents, grandparents, partners and friends all welcome. Let me know roughly how many people are coming.

What should I wear under my gown?

Something you feel great in, in solid colours that won’t clash with your hood. Comfortable shoes for walking around campus help too.
