Questions

Graduation details

Can we shoot on a different day from the ceremony?

Certainly. Many graduates prefer a calmer day before or after the ceremony, when campus is quieter. Just bring your gown.

Can family and friends be included?

Yes, and I’d encourage it. Let me know roughly how many people will join so I can include them in your quote.

What should I wear under my gown?

Something classic in a solid colour that complements your hood, and comfortable shoes for walking between locations.

How do I get a quote?

Send your ceremony date, preferred location and the number of people through the enquiry page and I’ll reply with a tailored quote.
