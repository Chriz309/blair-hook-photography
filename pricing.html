FAQ

Before you book

Do you have a studio?

No — all my sessions are on location. Potchefstroom has beautiful spots, from campus and gardens to open fields at golden hour, and we can also shoot at your home or venue.

Do you travel outside Potchefstroom?

Yes, I’m happy to travel. Let me know where you’d like to shoot and I’ll include any travel costs in your quote.

How long does a session take?

As long as it needs to. I don’t work on a stopwatch — most sessions naturally run until we’ve got everything you hoped for, plus a few surprises.

How many photos will I get?

Lots. I deliver every good frame fully edited rather than a small selection, so you get a big, varied gallery to choose your favourites from.

What should we wear?

Outfits you feel confident in, with colours that complement each other rather than match exactly. Avoid big logos and busy patterns. I’m always happy to give advice before the shoot.

How do I book?

Use the booking page or WhatsApp me on 072 963 3580. Tell me the type of session, your date and how many people, and I’ll confirm the details with you.
