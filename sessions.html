Questions

Good to know

How much does a session cost?

Every session is quoted individually, based on the occasion, the number of people and the location. Send a quick request with your details and I’ll come back to you with a tailored quote.

Do you have a studio?

No. Every session is photographed on location, which I believe gives more natural, beautiful results. Potchefstroom has wonderful spots, and I’m happy to come to your home or venue.

Do you travel outside Potchefstroom?

Yes. Tell me where you’d like to shoot and I’ll include any travel in your quote.

How long does a session take?

As long as it needs to. I don’t work against a clock; we finish when you have the photographs you were hoping for.

What should we wear?

Timeless, well-fitted outfits in complementary tones work best: think soft neutrals, rich solids and natural fabrics, without large logos or busy prints. I’m always happy to advise before your session.

How do I book?

Use the enquiry page or message me on WhatsApp at 072 963 3580. Share your occasion, date and the number of people, and we’ll take it from there.
